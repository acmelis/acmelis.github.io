My name is Anita Melis, I am based in Berlin but move around during the year. When I'm not in Berlin you might find me in Sardinia.

At 14 I wanted to become a photographer. I decided to ask for a summer employment at the café / organic food store next door. They took me and two months and many coffees and sandwiches mastered later I bought myself a digital Canon camera. Not long ago I gifted it to a friend, it still works.

While waitressing at the café I trained in a youth circus. It started when I was 8 and until the age of 16 I continued training, mainly trapeze and acrobatics. So, by chance two friends and I received the opportunity to train kids in acrobatics for 10 months when I was 15. We choreographed a show including hoops, curtains, and parkour paths. The class performed a beautiful show at the end of the year.

After 10 months spent in the UK as an exchange student at 16 I returned a different person. One year at an all girls performing arts school in an all girls household, waitressing in an italian restaurant in a small town in the East Midlands taught me how to iron, how to prepare lunch boxes, how to fight, how to dance, to act, to interpret; it taught me about the morning after pill, the monotony of walking to school for 40 minutes under the cold rain, not to get estranged at "yo'allrigh luv?", to drink my black tea with milk, to count my wages in pounds, and how to enjoy life.

These are just some of the experiences which have shaped me in my early years.

Today I have professional experience in the following fields:

- ### Project- and Event-Management:
Since many years I organise events and campaigns on Democracy, Inclusion and Antidiscrimination for public institutions in Berlin, Brandenburg and other regions in Germany. 

- ### Hospitality and Gastronomy:
I am at home in Gastronomy, Hospitality Management, Service, and Inclusive Event Management. Starting in Cafés and Catering Businesses to Restaurants and Bar Tending, to Cultural Venues with high class Service, I love working in Gastronomy. It's an art, and my highest professional expression of care.

- #### Translation:
I fluently speak German, Italian, English, and French. Some Sardinian, Spanish and a little Russian, discovered in university but used very little. I have experience in translating websites, short stories, poems, as well as in the field of intercultural interpreting.
